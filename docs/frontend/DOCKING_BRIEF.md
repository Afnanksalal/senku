# Docking workspace brief

Senku learners choose a curated teaching protein and small molecule, run a bounded search, then compare estimated poses against the deposited bound molecule. This is an experiment workspace, not a marketing page. Setup and results share a desktop grid; on phones setup precedes the result and all actions are native accessible controls. The primary action is Find poses. Successful runs retain their exact settings and seed, with independent pose selection, crystal-overlay and search-box controls. Tables and coordinate downloads remain available without3D.

Keep the mascot, existing palette/type, original icons and clear form rhythm. Secondary search detail is disclosed rather than placed in the primary path. No arbitrary protein upload or search-box editing. Scores are estimates, not measured binding strengths; the protein stays rigid. Automatic Senku reflection is grounded in an actual saved run, charged once under a stable request key, and cannot hide or replace numerical results on failure. No provider branding.
