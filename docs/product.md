# Product and learning model

Source: masterplan sections 1-6, 14, 17-20, 28, 30-31. Status: confirmed product intent; launch choices are recorded in [launch-brief.md](launch-brief.md).

## Product thesis

An accessible computational-science laboratory with a playful learning experience. The durable value is a reviewed experiment catalog, shared Learn/Lab runtime, device-adaptive visualization, reproducibility, and evidence-grounded orchestration. XP, streaks, and rewards support learning; they do not replace it. BioForge is integrated as Senku Scientific Core, not a second product or brand.

Biology is first. Future chemistry, physics, ecology/environmental science, and electronics should extend the registry and schemas without duplicating the interface architecture.

## Audiences

| Audience | Need |
|---|---|
| School students | Approachable guided experiments and explanations |
| University learners | Model detail, plots, assumptions, reproducible comparisons |
| Teachers | Reviewed templates, assignments, class progress, misconception insight |
| Science clubs | Cooperative challenges and shared exploration |
| Curious learners | Free-play experimentation |
| Research-oriented learners | Transparent demonstrations with explicit validity limits |

Launch serves school and college learners with concept-first content independent of a syllabus, in English initially. Exact age bands remain unspecified. Guests can explore; accounts enable saved cloud progress.

## Learning loop

Subject map -> guided mission -> prediction -> manipulate bounded parameters -> inspect model outputs -> explain and reflect -> earn learning credit -> open the same experiment in Lab -> compare runs -> inspect provenance -> export/replay.

Learn and Lab preserve experiment identity, model and engine versions, parameters, seed where supported, and run provenance. Different controls or visual detail must not secretly substitute a different scientific model.

## Product surfaces

| Surface | Job | Primary objects |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Resume and choose a daily mission | Progress, recent runs, recommendations, companion |
| Learn / World Map | Follow prerequisites and build understanding | Subject, chapter, mission, checkpoint, mastery |
| Interactive Lesson | Predict, manipulate, measure, explain | Experiment version, controls, result, reflection |
| Lab | Investigate with full approved controls | Model, runs, artifacts, diagnostics, comparisons |
| Challenges | Solve a scientific task | Scoring rubric, attempt, replay, optional cooperation |
| Discover | Ask a bounded research question | Investigation, evidence, task DAG, batches, report |
| Classroom / Teacher Studio | Create and assign reviewed learning activities | Template, assignment, class membership, progress |

Classroom and Teacher Studio are two views of the same educator capability. Discover is the investigation workspace, not an unrestricted chatbot. Real-time shared laboratories are required at launch. Private work and share links come first; public community/remixes and Teacher Studio follow the learner launch.

## Product vocabulary

Use mission, experiment, prediction, observation, model, run, result, compare, source, and explanation. Explain model assumptions in learner language. Keep container digests, queues, adapters, and worker IDs out of primary learner flows; expose technical provenance in the appropriate details view and exports.

## Hackathon positioning

Primary track: Make the Invisible Visible. Supporting tracks: Make Room for Play, Works Anyway, Kill the Busywork. The proof is an integrated journey, not four disconnected screens. Sections 17 and 30 propose different opening experiments; see decisions.

## Success

A learner completes a substantive mission, opens the same model in Lab, compares two actual results, inspects provenance, finishes a cached offline lesson, and views an AI interpretation grounded in real run outputs and retrievable sources. The expanded demo also includes a validated pathway run, public molecular structure, and teacher workflow. Pathway composition and structure exploration remain learner-platform scope; teacher tools follow later. Learners choose their entry experiment, guided learning and free labs are immediately accessible, and the AI companion stays quiet unless summoned.
